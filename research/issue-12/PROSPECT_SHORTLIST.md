## Candidate order

Order reflects apparent workflow fit, direct decision path, and credible use/distribution context. It does **not** rank candidates by views or presumed content weakness.

### 1. Hauling Success Podcast / Polyak Consulting — SCREEN-2026-09-01

- **Public evidence:** Official show page describes new episodes every two weeks, distributed through YouTube and Spotify. Its September 15, 2026 episode is a 46-minute interview about trucking legal risks, broker liability, and FMCSA compliance. Earlier episodes cover fuel surcharge calculations, cost-per-mile thinking, DOT driver files, and fleet financials.
- **Plausible fit:** These subjects involve rules, costs, and processes that may benefit from clear visual explanation. The host operates a consulting business and course with an existing small-carrier audience and commercial offering.
- **Public source:** [Hauling Success episodes](https://polyakconsulting.com/podcast); [Polyak Consulting](https://polyakconsulting.com/).
- **Unknowns to establish manually:** Whether the show has a recurring visual-content bottleneck; whether visual derivatives add value beyond its current editing; whether the decision-maker has source and distribution authority; whether it will accept the $300 experimental pilot and measurement permissions.
- **Screening judgment:** Best initial fit hypothesis. No evidence yet that it needs or would buy this service.

### 2. Truck Yoo Podcast — SCREEN-2026-09-02

- **Public evidence:** Its official site describes a trucking-business podcast with 100+ episodes, YouTube and podcast distribution, a free owner-operator community, and a host with trucking-company and diesel-shop experience. The site reports 29,000 YouTube subscribers and 4.5 million views; treat those as self-published figures, not independent validation.
- **Plausible fit:** Truck ownership, operating costs, maintenance, and profitability contain concrete subjects that may be easier to explain with original diagrams or visualized examples. The community and education offer provide a plausible audience/value path.
- **Public source:** [Truck Yoo Podcast](https://truckyoo.com/); [official contact page](https://truckyoo.com/contact-us).
- **Unknowns to establish manually:** Current publishing cadence and existing short-form workflow; whether the team wants the proposed kind of transformation; decision-maker, source authority, budget, and measurement access.
- **Screening judgment:** Strong second prospect. The established channel may also have an existing production process, so verify the gap before presenting an offer.

### 3. This Week in Trucking / Bobtail — SCREEN-2026-09-03

- **Public evidence:** Bobtail positions the show around cost per mile, rates, profitability, and small-carrier operations. Its public podcast page lists episodes about fleet growth, maintenance costs, and small-carrier operations; it also distributes content through a blog and YouTube.
- **Plausible fit:** The subjects fit the visual-explanation hypothesis and Bobtail has a clear commercial connection to trucking businesses.
- **Public source:** [Bobtail’s This Week in Trucking page](https://www.bobtail.com/blog/category/this-week-in-trucking/); [podcast feed](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2360279).
- **Unknowns to establish manually:** Whether Bobtail buys outside content services, who owns the budget and source rights, whether its current internal production makes the proposed intervention redundant, and whether a single $300 pilot fits its procurement process.
- **Screening judgment:** Keep as a later corporate prospect; it appears to have a more established content operation and a longer decision path than the two owner-led shows.

## First-contact preparation

Before any outreach, inspect the candidate’s current full-length episodes and public short-form outputs. Record whether a specific content problem is visible and cite the actual example. Do not infer “weak shorts” or a production gap from the existence of a long-form feed.

For the first target, use the recent episode topic only to personalize the question; do not download, transform, or publish any show material without explicit permission.

**Draft for manual use (not sent):**

> I saw your recent Hauling Success episode on trucking legal risks and FMCSA compliance. I’m testing a $300 pilot that turns one authorized episode into a small, human-reviewed multi-format package, including up to two cinematic short-form story derivatives and supporting written assets. For complex topics, the test is whether visual explanation adds enough usefulness beyond ordinary editing to justify the extra work. Your team would approve the assets and control any publishing; I’d measure production time, costs, revisions, and only the outcomes you authorize. There are no view, lead, or revenue guarantees. Would you be open to discussing whether one episode is a fit for that bounded test?

A prospect’s response may change the order. Preserve declines, no response, concerns, and reasons for rejection. Keep all real-world interaction, contact details, agreements, permissions, payment evidence, and raw customer content outside the public repository. Do not advance Issue #12 from this shortlist alone.
